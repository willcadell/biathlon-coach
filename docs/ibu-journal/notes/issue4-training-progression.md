# Training progression from junior to senior levels in biathlon
Kock (Univ. of Leipzig). Journal of Biathlon Coaching, Issue 4, Sep 2025, pp. 6-7. DOI 10.71006/sgmv2318. Read in full (2 pages). Retrospective data from 18 former elite German biathletes (7 women, 11 men), 1994/95-2013/14, with relay podiums at major events.

- Shots per year: juniors about 4,500-15,000 (most, over 60%, precision shooting); seniors about 12,000-22,000, of which 60-70% fired together with endurance training (skiing, roller-skiing, running) and the rest at rest. Male seniors shot about 4% more than U23 and 15% more than juniors, mostly because of more technical and in-competition shots. Precision shot numbers were similar across age groups: technical shooting at rest stays important throughout.
- Total training volume rose from junior to senior for men (about 230 hours), mostly more specific volume; no difference for the women in this sample (women seniors under-represented). Intensity distribution stayed pyramid-shaped: mostly low intensity.
- Practical points (author): keep a high number of precision shots; they build control of technical elements (body and rifle sway, triggering) under physical preload; develop technical shooting at rest first, then bring in physical preload step by step; raise the number of competitions per year to carry skills into racing.

Season relevance: supports a plan where shooting at rest and low intensity dominates the base period, with shooting under load added progressively, and more race-like practice as competitions approach.
