# Issue 6 (June 2026): proposals

Checked 2026-10-17 by hand, from the Issuu copy of the article (the journal site
shows only the abstract).

**Article:** Köykkä, Sattlecker & Laaksonen, "From research to practice: Optimising
shooting performance in elite biathlon", *Journal of Biathlon Coaching*, Issue 6,
pp. 28-33, DOI 10.71006/VDOL4813. Full text read (six pages).
https://issuu.com/biathlonworld/docs/ibu_jobc_issue_6_july_2026_journal-_556c0beddf815f

**What it is:** structured interviews with five international coaches (Mazet,
Kähkönen, Hagström, Amat, Torneus-Kulstad), not an experiment. The evidence is
expert practice, so treat it as practitioner consensus. It ends in ten
recommendations for coaches.

## The ideas, and what each could mean for the app

### 1. Keep the technique constant, change only the breathing (strongest finding)
Position, rifle setup, aiming, trigger control and follow-through should stay the
same at every intensity. What changes with effort is the breathing: its timing and
depth, while the last stretch of breathing before the shot stays the same.
- **Change to an existing drill: `breathing-pause`.** It gives one fixed timing for
  everyone. Add a step: at a higher heart rate, take deeper or extra breaths before
  the pause, but keep the same final sequence into the shot.
- **Change to `combo-intervals`:** say what to watch in the combo: only the breathing
  should change from the calm bouts, not the trigger or the aim.

### 2. Treat the range approach as a skill
Arrive with breathing feeling normal and under control, not as fast as possible.
Ease the skiing pace over the last 200-300 m to get there. Skiing too hard raised
rifle movement, lengthened aiming time and delayed heart-rate recovery. Add a short
mental transition: attention on the present, a read of the conditions.
- **Change to `range-entry`:** add the 200-300 m easing and the mental transition.
- **Change to the `cold_first_shot` finding's cause text:** name arrival state
  (breathing and heart rate) alongside the entry routine.

### 3. Shooting is periodised like the physical training
Start the season with shooting at rest and at low intensity (foundations, rifle
settings, movement patterns under stable conditions), then bring in moderate and
high intensity step by step, and toward the competition season move from trying
things to repeating established routines. One coach: easy and long, intense and
short, and recovery as well.
- **New text in the plan's help or a note on `progression`:** do not add pressure
  until the basics are steady, and include easy days. This matches the existing
  "nothing broken, so add pressure" logic and the IBU catalogue's rule of making
  drills harder only once stable.

### 4. Individualise the rifle setup, with care
Cheekpiece, hand stop, grip, butt plate (and standing grip), sight set-up and
natural point of aim, tuned to the athlete for comfort and alignment. Zero in
calm, windless conditions. Coaches made few or no big changes to equipment before
the Olympics; one planned small, step-by-step changes (for example barrel height)
and testing a change only where it should help.
- **Change to `zero-check`:** add "calm, windless conditions" to the first step.
- **Possible new drill, "Rifle fit check":** natural point of aim, then butt plate
  and grip comfort. The app cannot see setup, so keep it a checklist for the athlete
  and coach, and say not to change equipment close to an important race.

### 5. Train the conditions, not only the technique
Fatigue, pressure, wind and altitude (deeper or extra breaths before and between
shots at altitude). Competition routines built through simulation.
- Already covered by `combo-intervals`, `progression` and the wind finding. No change
  beyond a line on altitude breathing if the app gains a venue field (it has none).

### 6. Objective feedback together with athlete autonomy
Match how much feedback to the athlete: some want detail, some do better with room
to reflect. This is how the app already behaves (numbers plus coach notes). No change.

## Not usable by the app
Mindset, crowd and venue-specific preparation for the 2030 Games: not measurable
from target photos or metal bouts, and not something a drill card can carry.

## New advice rules
None. Everything above is about breathing, arrival state and setup, which the advice
engine cannot see. The only data-linked item is the `cold_first_shot` finding, whose
wording could change (idea 2).

## Suggested order if you act on this
1. `breathing-pause` and `range-entry` step changes (small, clearly supported).
2. `zero-check` calm-conditions wording.
3. `cold_first_shot` cause text.
4. Rifle fit check drill, only if wanted.
